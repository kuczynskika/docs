# 14-day free trial overview

Try MessageDesk free for 14 days. You'll pick a plan and add a card up front, and you won't be charged until the trial ends.

---

## TL;DR

- **Credit card required.** You'll need a card to create a MessageDesk account and to start your trial.
- **You pick a plan up front.** Your **14-day trial** runs against the plan you choose. See [Pricing](/resources/pricing) for what each one includes.
- **Messaging during trial:** Works after **carrier registration** is complete.
- **At day 14:** Billing **begins on the plan you selected** unless you **cancel** before the trial ends.
- **Cancel anytime** during the 14 days and you won't be charged.

<Note>
  **Twilio BYOC users:** If you connect existing **Twilio** numbers, you're still exempt from carrier registration inside MessageDesk because Twilio handles it on their side. Twilio BYOC no longer includes a free-seat exemption. You'll need a **paid subscription with at least one user seat** to connect Twilio numbers.
</Note>

## 14-day free trial: when it starts and what it includes

### How your trial starts

Your 14-day trial begins once you **select a plan** and **enter a credit card**. You'll hit this during signup, and again if you're prompted while **adding teammates** or **connecting phone lines**.

### Trial feature access

Your trial gives you the plan you picked, so what's available depends on that choice.

| Feature / Area                                 | Trial access                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Messaging**                                  | **Limited** until **carrier registration** is completed |
| **Phone line management**                      | Yes                                                     |
| **Teammate management**                        | Yes                                                     |
| **Media & attachments**                        | Yes                                                     |
| **Templates & tags**                           | Yes                                                     |
| **Labels**                                     | Yes                                                     |
| **Comments & mentions**                        | Yes                                                     |
| **Contacts**                                   | Yes                                                     |
| **Twilio integration**                         | Yes                                                     |
| **Roles & permissions (user access controls)** | Yes                                                     |
| **Relays (automations)**                       | Pro and Enterprise plans                                |
| **Zapier integration**                         | Pro and Enterprise plans                                |
| **Text broadcasts**                            | Pro and Enterprise plans                                |

<Info>
  **Messaging & compliance:** If you're using a local (10DLC) or toll-free number, you'll need to [complete the carrier registration process](/carrier-registration/overview) before sending messages.
</Info>

<Note>
  **Trialing on Team but want to test Relays or Zapier?** Those are Pro features, so a Team trial won't include them. Switch plans in **Billing**, or [contact sales](/contact-us) before your 14 days run out. See [Pricing](/resources/pricing) for the full breakdown.
</Note>

## What happens when your trial ends

### Choose a plan (recommended before day 14)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to your **Account** (user icon) → **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Click **Choose a plan**, compare options, and select the one that fits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm payment">
    Enter or confirm your payment method and **confirm subscription**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Check the seat minimum before you pick.** The **Team** plan bills a minimum of **3 user seats**, so that's what you'll pay at day 14 even with a smaller team. **Pro** starts at 1 seat. See [Pricing](/resources/pricing) for the full plan comparison and what each tier includes.
</Note>

### If you do nothing

- If you started a trial by selecting a plan and adding a card, **billing will begin automatically** for that plan at the end of your 14 days.
- You can **cancel anytime during the trial** to avoid charges.

<Warning>
  **To avoid charges:** Cancel before your 14-day period ends.
</Warning>

### Need more time?

[**Contact sales**](/contact-us) to discuss **trial extension** options.

## FAQs

**Do I need a credit card to create a MessageDesk account?**\
Yes. A card is required to create an account and to start your free 14-day trial.

**Will I be charged during the trial?**\
No. Billing starts at the end of your 14 days on the plan you selected. Cancel before then and you won't be charged.

**Can I cancel during the trial?**\
Yes. **Cancel anytime** during your 14 days with **no charges**.

**What happens to my data if I don't upgrade?**\
We **preserve your data** indefinitely. You can return and upgrade later.

**Can I switch plans after my trial?**\
Yes. You can **change plans anytime** after upgrading.
