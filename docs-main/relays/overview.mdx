# How to automate with Relays

Relays are MessageDesk's workflow engine. They listen for **events**, evaluate **conditions**, and run **actions** so the right conversation reaches the right person at the right time.

<Warning>
  **Before you start.** Relays are desktop-only, and they're on the **Pro** and **Enterprise** plans ([Pricing](/resources/pricing)). Only **Admins** get access by default. Admins can grant **Relay Management** to other roles in [Team Management](/settings/workspace-settings/team-management).
</Warning>

## Why use Relays?

- **Scale without chaos:** Auto-assign and auto-label to keep your Inbox tidy as volume grows.
- **Respond faster:** Send **after-hours** and **first-touch** replies automatically.
- **Reduce busywork:** Route by **phone line**, **label**, or **teammate** so work starts in the right place.
- **Stay consistent:** Standardize rules so you don't miss handoffs.

<Note>
  **Relays or Zapier?** Start with Relays when the whole workflow lives in MessageDesk. They're first-party, go deeper into the product, and don't depend on an outside account. Reach for [Zapier](/integrations/zapier) when you need to touch another app, or when you need branching, filters, and multi-step logic. The two share a vocabulary on purpose, so what you learn in one carries over.
</Note>

## What you can do with Relays

Relays cover four kinds of work:

- **Answer without a person.** After-hours replies, keyword replies, and a first-touch question for texts from unsaved numbers.
- **Get the thread to the right owner.** Route by phone line, keyword, label, or contact property. Assign the first teammate who replies. Reset assignment when a thread closes or reopens.
- **Keep the Inbox honest.** Auto-label, auto-close threads nobody needs to touch, and force a thread back to **Unread** so an auto-reply doesn't look like a handled conversation.
- **Talk to your other systems.** Push messages, closes, assignments, and opt-outs out by webhook. Let a CRM or form send a webhook in and text a contact through MessageDesk.

Three triggers don't run on a conversation. **Contact Created**, **Contact Updated**, and **Inbound Webhook Received** fire against a contact, so you can text a welcome the moment someone is saved, or sync a property from another system. Use [Event Data](/relays/building-blocks#event-data) to pull values from the event into your messages instead of typing fixed text.

<Tip>
  Every template is customizable, and every one arrives paused. Nothing runs until you toggle it **On**.
</Tip>

## How Relays work (at a glance)

![Relay Example Pn](/images/relay-example.png)

**Relays = Triggers → Conditions → Actions**

- **Triggers:** When it runs.\
  Fourteen triggers, from **Message Received** to **Opt Out Created**. See [Relay building blocks](/relays/building-blocks#triggers) for the full list.
- **Conditions:** Where/when it applies.\
  _Examples:_\
  • **My phone number equals** _Support_\
  • **Is anonymous conversation** is **true**\
  • **Is unassigned conversation** is **true**\
  • **Active hours** with **Trigger outside of business hours** enabled
- **Actions:** What happens.\
  _Examples:_\
  • **Assign teammates to the conversation**\
  • **Add labels to the conversation**\
  • **Reply to message** (with templates, tags, media, and an optional Delay)\
  • **Open the conversation** or **Close the conversation**

You can **pause** and **resume** any Relay without deleting it.

<Note>
  **Multiple Relays on one event.** When several Relays share the same trigger, **all matching Relays run** in the **Order** you set on the Relays list, top to bottom. Reorder Relays to control sequencing when conditions overlap. For example, run a specific keyword auto-reply before a generic after-hours reply. See [Order your Relays](/relays/create-edit#1a-order-your-relays).
</Note>

## Relay templates

MessageDesk ships 58 templates, grouped by outcome in the app. Groups and template cards expand and collapse, so you can scan every outcome at once and open just the one you need.

<Tip>
  See [Relay templates](/relays/templates) for all 58, the builds behind them organized by trigger, and an industry-by-industry starting point. For every trigger, condition, and action, see [Relay building blocks](/relays/building-blocks).
</Tip>

<Note>
  **An auto-reply isn't a handled conversation.** A Relay reply moves the thread to **Replied**, which takes it out of the Unread filter, but nobody has read it yet. See [Relays and conversation status](/relays/create-edit#relays-and-conversation-status).
</Note>
