# Zapier integration (Beta)

Connect MessageDesk to thousands of other apps using **Zapier**.\
Start a workflow when a message arrives, a contact changes, or someone calls your number. Then send texts, log comments, look up contacts, and keep your CRM in sync without writing code.

<Note>
  **Plan required.** The Zapier integration is available on the **Pro** and **Enterprise** plans. See [Pricing](/resources/pricing) for the full comparison.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Permissions required.** To build a Zap that authenticates with your MessageDesk account, your user role needs **Editor** or **Owner** on the **Developer** feature in [Team Management](/settings/workspace-settings/team-management). **Admins** have this by default. **Viewers** can't authenticate Zapier under their own account, so any Zap a Viewer builds or owns won't run.

  Each Zap runs under the **MessageDesk account that authenticated it**. If a Zap stops working, check the authenticated user's role first.
</Note>

## What you can automate

- **When a message is received** → Create helpdesk tickets, send Slack or Teams notifications, or log messages to Google Sheets.
- **When a message is sent** → Update CRM records, trigger a follow-up task, or post delivery updates.
- **When someone calls your number** → Look up the conversation, draft a reply with an AI step, and text them back.
- **When a contact is created or changed** → Push the record to your CRM so both systems stay in sync.
- **When a contact opts out** → Flag the matching CRM record as unsubscribed so your sequences stop.
- **From your CRM, forms, or scheduling tools** → Create or update contacts in MessageDesk and send personalized texts.
- **From payments or invoicing systems** → Send SMS confirmations when a customer pays or an invoice goes out.
- **From your HR or scheduling system** → Keep your employee and crew contact lists current. See [Contact and roster sync](#contact-and-roster-sync).

<Note>
  **Requirements:**

  - A [Zapier](https://zapier.com) account and a connected MessageDesk workspace.
  - Outbound texting via Zapier follows the same [**registration**](/carrier-registration/overview), [**delivery**](/messaging/delivery-status-errors), and [**compliance**](/settings/workspace-settings/compliance-center) standards as sending directly from MessageDesk.
</Note>

## How Zapier works

A Zap has three kinds of steps.

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Trigger** | The event that starts your Zap, like a message arriving in MessageDesk. |
| **Action** | The task Zapier performs after the trigger fires, like sending a message or creating a contact. |
| **Search** | A lookup in the middle of a Zap. Find a record so later steps can use it, and optionally create it if it doesn't exist. |

<Note>
  **Every MessageDesk step starts with your workspace.** The first field on each trigger, action, and search is **Workspace Name**. It's a dropdown built from the MessageDesk account you connected, so you pick your workspace instead of typing it. The field tables below only list what comes after that.
</Note>

| **Opt-Out Created** | Fires when a phone number opts out, whether by STOP keyword, manual opt-out, or the Create Opt Out action. |
| **Opt-Out Deleted** | Fires when an opt-out is removed and that number can receive messages again. |
| **Call Received** | Fires when someone calls one of your MessageDesk numbers. |

## Available MessageDesk actions

| Action | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Send Message** | Sends a text or MMS to one recipient. |
| **Send Group Message** | Sends one message to a small group, as a single group thread. |
| **Send Broadcast Message** | Sends a message to a list of recipients, each as a private 1-on-1 thread. |
| **Create or Update Contact** | Adds a contact or updates an existing one, matched by phone number. |

</Note>

### Contact Created

Fires when someone creates a contact in your workspace. The payload includes the contact's [custom fields](/settings/workspace-settings/custom-fields).

Use it to mirror new MessageDesk contacts into your CRM, or to kick off a welcome text.

<Note>
  **CSV imports don't fire this trigger.** A bulk import creates one import event rather than one event per row, and Zapier doesn't listen for it. So importing 10,000 contacts won't burn 10,000 Zap tasks. If you need imported contacts in another system, export them or sync from the other side.
</Note>

---

### Contact Updated

Fires when any field on a contact changes. That includes the name, phone number, email, and [custom fields](/settings/workspace-settings/custom-fields).

There's no separate trigger for custom field changes. One trigger covers every edit, because most people think of it as "the contact changed" rather than "field seven changed."

---

### Contact Deleted

Fires when someone deletes a contact from your workspace. Use it to close out or archive the matching record elsewhere.

<Note>
  This is a trigger only. There's no matching action to delete a MessageDesk contact from another app. See [Contact and roster sync](#contact-and-roster-sync).
</Note>

---

### Opt-Out Created

Fires when a phone number opts out of messaging. It covers every path: a STOP keyword, a manual opt-out in the Inbox, and the Create Opt Out action.

<Note>
  **Opt-outs belong to a phone number, not to a contact.** One contact can have several numbers, and a conversation doesn't need a contact record at all. So the payload tells you which number opted out, not which person. If you need contact details, add a **Find or Create Contact** search after the trigger.

  See [Anonymous contacts and blocked opt-outs](/contacts/anonymous-opt-out-blocked) for how MessageDesk handles numbers with no contact behind them.
</Note>

---

### Opt-Out Deleted

Fires when an opt-out is removed, which means that number can receive messages again. Pair it with Opt-Out Created to keep your CRM's subscription status accurate in both directions.

---

### Call Received

Fires when someone calls one of your MessageDesk numbers.

This is the front half of the missed-call text-back recipe. Chain it with **Find or Create Conversation**, an AI step, and **Send Message** to answer a call with a text in seconds.

<Warning>
  **Call Received only works on numbers where MessageDesk handles both voice and texting.** That means numbers you [purchased through MessageDesk](/get-started/phone-numbers/purchase-new) and numbers you fully ported to us, including [fully ported Google Voice numbers](/migration/google-voice).

  It won't fire on hosted numbers, where we text-enable your line but your original carrier still handles calls.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The trigger fires on the call itself. It doesn't tell you whether the call was answered, missed, or completed, and it doesn't include a recording or transcript.
</Note>

---

### Comment Created

Fires when a teammate posts an internal [comment](/inbox/comments-mentions) on a conversation. Use it to push escalations into Slack, or to open a ticket when someone flags a thread.

It won't fire on customer-facing messages. Those are covered by Message Received and Message Sent.

## Actions

### Send Message

Sends an outbound text or MMS from MessageDesk. Each Zap run sends one message.

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Workspace Number to Send From** | ✅ | Pick the sending line from a dropdown of your workspace's connected numbers. |
| **Recipient Number** | ✅ | The phone number to text. |
| **Message Text** | ✅ | The body of your message. |
| **Media URL** | ❌ | A publicly accessible link to an image. Sends the message as MMS. |

![Send Message](/images/send-message.png)

<Warning>
  Messages sent through Zapier count toward your MessageDesk usage and must follow the same carrier and compliance rules as messages sent from your Inbox.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **MessageDesk enforces opt-outs automatically.** If the recipient has opted out in MessageDesk, the **Send Message** action fails and no text goes out, even if the Zap itself runs successfully on Zapier's side. You can't bypass opt-out enforcement. See the [Compliance Center](/settings/workspace-settings/compliance-center) for how MessageDesk captures and manages opt-outs.

  This works in your favor when you automate outreach off CRM activity. You don't need to check opt-out status in your Zap first, because MessageDesk checks it on every send.
</Note>

---

### Send Group Message

Sends one message to a small group as a single group thread, the same way [group messaging](/inbox/multi-select-quick-actions) works in the Inbox. Everyone sees everyone else's replies.

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Workspace Number to Send From** | ✅ | Pick the sending line from a dropdown of your workspace's connected numbers. |
| **Recipients** | ✅ | Up to seven phone numbers, plus your sending number. |
| **Message Text** | ✅ | The body of your message. |
| **Media URL** | ❌ | A publicly accessible link to an image. Sends the message as MMS. |

<Note>
  **Group message or broadcast?** A group message puts everyone in one thread and caps at seven recipients. A [broadcast](#send-broadcast-message) goes out to a list, and each reply comes back as a private 1-on-1 conversation. Pick the group message when the recipients should see each other.
</Note>

---

### Send Broadcast Message

Sends a message to a list of recipients. Each person gets it as a private 1-on-1 message, and replies open individual threads. This is the Zapier version of a [broadcast](/messaging/overview#1-to-many-text-message-broadcasts).

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Workspace Number to Send From** | ✅ | Pick the sending line from a dropdown of your workspace's connected numbers. |
| **Recipients** | ✅ | The list of phone numbers to send to. |
| **Message Text** | ✅ | The body of your message. |
| **Media URL** | ❌ | A publicly accessible link to an image. Sends the message as MMS. |

<Warning>
  **Broadcasts depend on your plan.** If your plan doesn't include broadcasts, or you've hit a volume limit, the action fails with a clear error and nothing sends. MessageDesk enforces this on our side, so a Zap can't route around it. Check [Pricing](/resources/pricing) for what your plan covers.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Broadcasts sent through Zapier follow the same compliance rules as broadcasts sent from the app. Segment your list, keep the message relevant, and include opt-out language.
</Note>

Creates or updates a contact in your selected workspace, matched by phone number. Good for syncing leads, form submissions, or new customer signups.

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Contact Phone Number** | ✅ | The contact's phone number. |
| **First Name / Last Name** | ❌ | Optional contact name fields. |

| **Custom fields** | ❌ | Your workspace's [custom fields](/settings/workspace-settings/custom-fields), pulled in automatically so you can set typed values. |

<Note>
  **Matching is by phone number.** If the number coming out of your other app doesn't match what's on the MessageDesk contact, you'll get a second contact instead of an update. When a source app stores several numbers per record, map the one you actually text.

### Create Comment

Posts an internal comment on a conversation. Use it to log notes from other apps, pull a teammate in, or leave context before someone picks up the thread.

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Workspace Number** | ✅ | The phone number tied to the workspace conversation. |
| **Contact Number** | ✅ | The contact's phone number, used to find the conversation. |
| **Comment Text** | ✅ | The text body of your comment. |
| **Mention** | ❌ | Pick a teammate from a dropdown of your workspace users. They get a normal mention notification. |
| **Media URL** | ❌ | A publicly accessible link to an image. |

![Create Comment](/images/create-comment.png)

<Note>
  **No conversation yet? The action creates one.** Earlier versions reported a successful run while the comment never showed up in the Inbox, because there was no thread to attach it to. Now the conversation gets created and the comment lands where you'd expect.

  Comments post to the conversation, not to each other. There's no comment threading.
</Note>

### Create Opt Out

Opts one or more phone numbers out of messaging. The result matches what a STOP keyword does, so every downstream suppression rule applies.

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Phone Numbers** | ✅ | One or more numbers to opt out. |

Use it when someone unsubscribes in your CRM, your helpdesk, or a preference center, and that decision needs to reach MessageDesk.

Opting out a number that's already opted out succeeds quietly, so you don't need to check first. MessageDesk records the opt-out source as Zapier in your workspace audit trail.

<Warning>
  **This action fires the Opt-Out Created trigger.** If you also run a Zap on that trigger, the two can keep waking each other up. See [Actions fire triggers](#actions-fire-triggers) before you build both directions.
</Warning>

## Searches

A search looks up a MessageDesk record in the middle of a Zap so later steps can use it. Both searches can create the record when nothing matches, using the same **create if it doesn't exist yet** checkbox Zapier uses everywhere else.

### Find or Create Contact

Looks up a contact by phone number. Turn on the create option and it falls back to **Create or Update Contact**, so the create path accepts the same fields, custom fields included.

### Find or Create Conversation

Looks up a conversation by phone number, optionally scoped to one of your workspace numbers.

Two things make this more flexible than it sounds:

- **It works without a contact.** MessageDesk supports anonymous conversations, so you can find or create a thread for a phone number with no contact record attached and none created.
- **A conversation doesn't need messages.** You can create one before anyone has texted, which is useful if you label and organize threads ahead of time.

<Note>
  **No match isn't a failure.** When a search finds nothing and you haven't turned on the create option, the step returns empty instead of erroring. Add a Zapier Filter or Path after it to branch on whether the record exists.
</Note>

## What's in a trigger payload

MessageDesk trigger payloads carry the event itself and not much else. A Message Received payload is the message. A Comment Created payload is the comment. Related records don't ride along.

That's deliberate. Fat payloads get slow, they break when one join fails, and they force you to receive data you didn't ask for. If you need the contact behind a message, add a **Find or Create Contact** step and pull it in.

Two things worth knowing:

- **Contact references can be empty.** Conversations work without contact records, so any contact reference in a payload might be null. Build your Zap to handle that.
- **Contacts are the exception.** Contact payloads always include the custom fields map, because contacts are the one place the extra data is always worth carrying.

## Reliability and limits

Here's how delivery actually behaves, so you can decide what to trust it with.

### How triggers reach Zapier

MessageDesk triggers use **REST hooks**, not polling. We push the event to Zapier as it happens, so there's no polling interval to wait through and no schedule that varies by Zapier plan.

### We don't retry

**If we can't deliver an event to Zapier, that event is gone.** There's no retry, no backoff, and no queue holding it for later.

In practice this is rare, and it isn't the same as a Zap step failing. A Zap that runs and then errors on a later step can be replayed from your Zapier task history. An event that never reached Zapier at all never created a task, so there's nothing to replay.

<Warning>
  **Don't use Zapier as your system of record.** If a MessageDesk event has to reach another system every single time, without exception, Zapier alone isn't the right transport. Reconcile against MessageDesk on a schedule, or talk to us about your requirements.
</Warning>

### Duplicates and ordering

- **Events don't fire twice.** Each event delivers once.
- **There's no event ID or idempotency key** in the payload. If you need your own dedupe key, build one from fields in the payload.
- **Order isn't guaranteed.** Two events fired moments apart can arrive out of order. It's uncommon, but don't write a Zap that depends on Contact Created landing before Contact Updated.

### Rate limits

MessageDesk doesn't rate limit actions coming from Zapier, and Send Broadcast Message has no volume cap beyond what your plan includes. Normal carrier throughput still applies to anything you send, and so do your plan's [broadcast entitlements](/resources/pricing).

Because nothing throttles you on our side, a misconfigured Zap can do real work before you notice. See below.

### Actions fire triggers

**Every MessageDesk action fires the matching MessageDesk trigger.** MessageDesk is event-driven, so an action taken by a Zap looks the same as an action taken by a person.

| This action | Fires this trigger |
| --- | --- |
| Create or Update Contact | Contact Created, Contact Updated |
| Create Opt Out | Opt-Out Created |
| Create Comment | Comment Created |
| Send Message, Send Group Message, Send Broadcast Message | Message Sent |

There are no loop guards on our side. If one Zap's action feeds another Zap's trigger, they'll keep triggering each other until Zapier stops them.

<Warning>
  **Don't pair a MessageDesk action with the trigger it fires.** Two-way syncs are where this bites. A Zap that pushes MessageDesk contacts into your CRM, running alongside a Zap that pushes CRM contacts back into MessageDesk, is a cycle: each write fires an update, which triggers the other Zap, which writes again.

  Pick one direction and make it the authority. If you truly need both, put a Zapier Filter or Path in front of each action so a record that just arrived from the other system doesn't get written back.
</Warning>

### When something breaks

- **Errors show up in Zapier**, in the Zap's task history, with whatever detail the failing step returned. Validation errors name the value we couldn't read, and a broadcast on an ineligible plan returns an entitlement error.
- **MessageDesk has no Zapier activity view.** There's nowhere in your workspace to see Zap runs, successes, or failures. Zapier's task history is the only place to look.
- **We don't email you when a Zap breaks.** Zapier notifies you by default, based on your Zapier account's notification settings. Check those if a silent failure would hurt.

### Re-authentication

Your MessageDesk connection in Zapier needs to be re-authenticated **at least every 30 days**. It can happen sooner if something revokes the token, like a password reset on the MessageDesk account that authorized the connection.

A Zap with an expired connection stops running until someone reconnects it.

## Custom fields

Your workspace's [custom fields](/settings/workspace-settings/custom-fields) show up on both sides of a Zap.

- **On contact triggers**, custom fields and their values come through in the payload.
- **On contact actions**, MessageDesk pulls your custom fields into the Zap editor so you can map values into the right typed field instead of guessing at names.

Add a new custom field in MessageDesk and it shows up the next time you refresh the step in Zapier.

<Note>
  **Custom fields need version 2.0.** Earlier versions of the app didn't expose them at all. If your custom fields aren't appearing in the Zap editor, switch the step to **2.0** and refresh it.
</Note>

## Phone number formatting

You don't need a Formatter step. MessageDesk accepts the phone formats other apps hand you and normalizes them on our side, using the same rules as [contact imports](/contacts/create-import).

All of these reach the same contact:

- `(555) 123-4567`
- `555-123-4567`
- `15551234567`
- `+15551234567`

<Note>
  **MessageDesk treats numbers without a country code as US or Canada (+1).** If a number can't be parsed, the step fails with an error naming the value that caused it, so you can fix the mapping instead of hunting through a Zap history.
</Note>

<Note>
  **On version 1.x you needed a Formatter step** to convert numbers to E.164 before MessageDesk would accept them. If you have old Zaps carrying a Formatter step just for phone numbers, switch them to 2.0 and remove it.
</Note>

## Contact and roster sync

A common setup is to keep a list of people somewhere else, like an HR system, a scheduling tool, a project board, or a spreadsheet, and mirror it into MessageDesk contacts so your team can text them.

Zapier handles most of this well, with one gap.

| What you want | Supported? |
| --- | --- |
| Person added in the other app → contact in MessageDesk | ✅ **Create or Update Contact** |
| Person edited in the other app → contact updated | ✅ **Create or Update Contact**, matched on phone number |
| Contact changes in MessageDesk → push to the other app | ✅ **Contact Created**, **Contact Updated**, and **Contact Deleted** triggers |
| Person removed in the other app → contact removed | ❌ No Delete Contact action |

<Warning>
  **Run the sync in one direction.** The first two rows and the third row are opposite halves of the same loop. Because [actions fire triggers](#actions-fire-triggers), turning on both means each write sets off the other Zap. Pick the system that owns the roster and sync toward the other one.
</Warning>

### Working around the delete gap

There's no way to delete a MessageDesk contact from Zapier. Pick whichever of these fits how you work:

- **Mark instead of delete.** Add a [custom field](/settings/workspace-settings/custom-fields) like `Status` and have your Zap set it to `Inactive` with **Create or Update Contact**. Filter your inbox and broadcast lists on that field. This keeps message history intact, which is usually what you want anyway.
- **Opt out instead of delete.** Use **Create Opt Out** on the person's number. They stay in your workspace but can't be messaged, deliberately or accidentally.
- **Delete in bulk, by hand.** For a periodic cleanup, [export and re-import](/contacts/create-import) your contact list, or delete contacts directly in the Inbox.

<Note>
  **Source-app limits matter too.** Some apps don't publish a usable "item deleted" or "row removed" trigger to Zapier, so even the detection half may not be available on their side. Check the source app's trigger list before you plan a two-way sync.
</Note>

<Note>
  **For a large one-time roster load**, a [CSV import](/contacts/create-import) is faster and more reliable than a Zap, and it supports custom fields. Use Zapier to keep the list current afterward.
</Note>

## QuickBooks

Texting invoices, estimates, and payment confirmations is one of the most common reasons people connect MessageDesk to Zapier. A few specifics will save you time.

<Warning>
  **The legacy native QuickBooks integration is deprecated.** It only ever worked with MessageDesk Reach (V1), and we no longer maintain it. If you're on Inbox by MessageDesk, connect QuickBooks through Zapier instead. If you're on V1 and relying on the native integration, [contact support](mailto:support@messagedesk.com) about migrating.
</Warning>

### Add a Find Customer step

QuickBooks invoice and estimate triggers usually return a customer **reference**, not the customer's phone number. So a Zap that goes straight from "Invoice Created" to **Send Message** has nothing to send to.

Build it in this order:

1. **Trigger:** QuickBooks Online → *New Invoice* or *New Estimate*.
2. **Search:** QuickBooks Online → *Find Customer*, using the customer ID from the trigger. This is what gives you the phone number.
3. **Search:** MessageDesk → *Find or Create Contact*, mapping the phone number from step 2.
4. **Action:** MessageDesk → *Send Message*, with the invoice link or amount in the body.

### Payment status is unreliable as a trigger

QuickBooks doesn't always expose a clean "paid" state to Zapier. In practice the e-invoice status field can stay at `viewed` after an invoice is marked paid, and the `Paid in Full` state visible in the QuickBooks UI isn't available to Zapier at all.

If a payment-received Zap never fires, don't assume the problem is on the MessageDesk side. Try triggering on a linked payment or transaction record rather than on invoice status, and test against a real paid invoice before turning the Zap on.

### Field mappings can break after a QuickBooks update

QuickBooks periodically renames or restructures fields, including phone number fields like `primary phone free form number`. When that happens, existing Zaps fail with a mapping error rather than silently sending the wrong thing.

If a working QuickBooks Zap suddenly starts failing, open the step and re-map the phone field before troubleshooting anything else.

## Zapier or Relays?

Both automate MessageDesk. They're built for different jobs.

| Use | When |
| --- | --- |
| [**Relays**](/relays/overview) | The whole workflow lives in MessageDesk. Relays are first-party, go deeper into the product, and don't depend on an outside account. Start here. |
| **Zapier** | You need to reach another app, or you need multi-step logic, branching, filters, and paths that Relays doesn't cover. |

The two share a vocabulary on purpose. A Send Message action means the same thing in both places, so what you learn in one carries over.

<Note>
  **Why some things are Relays-only.** Zapier's actions cover programmatic tasks like sending a message, creating a contact, posting a comment, and opting someone out. Actions that mirror what a person does inside the Inbox, like assigning a conversation, changing its status, or adding and removing labels, are handled by Relays instead of duplicated in Zapier.

  You can still *react* to those events in Zapier, since the corresponding triggers fire normally.
</Note>

## Best practices

- ✅ **Include opt-out text:** "Reply STOP to opt out" for promotional or automated messages.
- ✅ **Pick the right send action:** one recipient uses **Send Message**, a small group that should see each other uses **Send Group Message**, and a list uses **Send Broadcast Message**.
- ✅ **Test with real data** before turning on your Zap.
- ✅ **Add a Find step when you need more than the event:** trigger payloads are intentionally small.
- ⚠️ **Sync in one direction:** pick the system that owns the record. Running a sync both ways creates a loop, because [actions fire triggers](#actions-fire-triggers).
- ⚠️ **Watch Zapier for failures:** MessageDesk has no Zapier activity view, so Zapier's task history and notification settings are your only warning.
- ✅ **Keep Zaps small and modular.** Several short Zaps are easier to debug than one long branching Zap, and a failure takes down less of your workflow.
- ✅ **Guard against duplicate sends.** Zapier's deduplication only stops the same trigger event from running twice. If a record can re-enter the same state later, log processed records in Zapier Tables or Google Sheets and filter on whether they're already there.
- 🧩 **Use filters and conditions:** limit your Zaps to relevant events, like only messages containing "Support."

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
| --- | --- |
| **Zapier only offers me "MessageDesk Reach"** | That's the legacy V1 app. The V2 app is invite-only and stays hidden until you accept the Zapier invitation, as an admin on your Zapier account. [Request access](https://messagedesk.typeform.com/to/C3cEgPAm), or ask support to resend the invite. Then search for **MessageDesk (Invite Only)** and pick **2.0**. |
| **I accepted the invite but the app still doesn't appear** | Confirm you accepted it while signed in to the same Zapier account you're building in, and that you're an admin on it. If you have more than one Zapier account, the invite only applies to the one that accepted it. |
| **My Zap doesn't have the new triggers or actions** | You're on an older version. Open the step and switch it to **2.0**. Deprecated versions keep running but won't get new functionality. |
| **Authentication fails when connecting to MessageDesk** | Reconnect your account in Zapier and confirm you're using the correct workspace and **version 2.0**. Authenticate with the email tied to your MessageDesk workspace. A personal address that isn't a member of the workspace won't authorize. |
| **Zaps stopped firing after I reconnected my account** | A reconnect can leave the old webhook subscription in place, so events go to a subscription no live Zap is listening on. Turning the Zap off and on again re-subscribes it. If it still doesn't fire, especially across several workspaces, [contact support](mailto:support@messagedesk.com) and we'll clear the stale subscriptions on our side. |
| **A phone number is rejected** | The error names the value that couldn't be read. MessageDesk treats numbers without a country code as US or Canada (+1). |
| **Message sent but not delivered** | Check [Delivery Status & Errors](/messaging/delivery-status-errors) for details. |
| **Send Message action fails for a specific contact** | Confirm the contact hasn't opted out. MessageDesk automatically blocks outbound messages to opted-out contacts, so the Zap action fails even though the Zap itself runs. See [Compliance Center](/settings/workspace-settings/compliance-center). |
| **Send Broadcast Message fails with a plan error** | Your plan doesn't include broadcasts, or you've hit a volume limit. MessageDesk enforces this on our side, so Zapier can't route around it. See [Pricing](/resources/pricing). |
| **MMS doesn't attach** | The media URL has to be publicly accessible. A link behind a login, a signed URL that's expired, or a file on an internal server won't work. See [Media](/messaging/media) for supported types and size limits. |
| **Call Received never fires** | The trigger only works on numbers where MessageDesk handles both voice and texting. Hosted numbers, where your original carrier still takes calls, won't fire it. |
| **Two of my Zaps keep triggering each other** | Every MessageDesk action fires the matching trigger, and there are no loop guards. If one Zap's action feeds the other Zap's trigger, turn one of them off and sync in a single direction. See [Actions fire triggers](#actions-fire-triggers). |
| **My contacts keep updating in a loop** | Same cause. A MessageDesk-to-CRM sync running alongside a CRM-to-MessageDesk sync writes back and forth forever. Pick the system that owns the record and sync toward the other one. |
| **My Zap stopped running and nothing changed** | Connections need re-authentication at least every 30 days, and sooner if a password reset revoked the token. Reconnect the MessageDesk account in Zapier. |
| **An event never showed up in Zapier at all** | MessageDesk doesn't retry failed deliveries, so an event that didn't reach Zapier never created a task and can't be replayed. See [Reliability and limits](#reliability-and-limits). |
| **Imported contacts didn't fire Contact Created** | That's expected. CSV imports create one bulk event instead of one per contact, and Zapier doesn't listen for it. |
| **A search step returns nothing** | An empty result isn't an error. Add a Filter or Path to branch on it, or turn on the create option so the search makes the record. |
| **Zap not triggering** | Verify the correct trigger and workspace setup. Send a test message to fire the event. |
| **Contact not updating correctly** | Check field mappings and confirm the contact's phone number matches existing records. Contacts match on phone number, so a mismatch creates a second contact instead of updating the first. |
| **I can't find an action to delete a contact** | There isn't one. Contact deletion can't be driven from Zapier. Mark the contact inactive with a custom field, or opt the number out instead. See [Contact and roster sync](#contact-and-roster-sync). |
| **My custom fields don't show up in the Zap editor** | Custom fields require **2.0**. Switch the step to 2.0 and refresh it. New fields added in MessageDesk appear the next time you refresh the step. |
| **A QuickBooks Zap suddenly started failing** | QuickBooks changes field names periodically, including phone fields. Open the step and re-map the phone number field. See [QuickBooks](#quickbooks). |
| **My QuickBooks payment Zap never fires** | QuickBooks doesn't reliably expose a paid state to Zapier. Trigger on a linked payment or transaction record instead of invoice status. See [QuickBooks](#quickbooks). |
| **My Zap built fine but isn't firing (or one Zap works and another doesn't)** | Check which **MessageDesk user authenticated** that specific Zap in Zapier. Each Zap runs under the user whose credentials connected the MessageDesk account. That user must have **Editor** or **Owner** on the **Developer** feature in [Team Management](/settings/workspace-settings/team-management). Common cause: an admin authenticated one Zap (works), and a teammate without Developer permission authenticated another (fails). Re-authenticate the failing Zap with a user who has the right permission, or have an admin grant the existing user **Developer = Editor**. |

## Still stuck?

If a Zap won't run and none of the above explains it, [email support](mailto:support@messagedesk.com) with:

- The Zap's name and the MessageDesk workspace it's connected to.
- The email address that authenticated the MessageDesk connection.
- A screenshot of the failing step from your Zap history, including the error text.

That's usually enough for us to reproduce it without a screen share.
