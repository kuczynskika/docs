Porting moves an entire phone number to MessageDesk. MessageDesk becomes the carrier of record, and the number leaves your current provider. You can submit the request yourself in-app, including signing the Letter of Authorization.

---

## Porting is not the same as hosting

Most teams who ask about porting actually want hosting. Read this before you start.

| | Hosting | Porting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What moves** | Texting only | Voice and texting |
| **Your current provider** | Keeps handling calls | No longer serves the number |
| **Reversible** | Stop hosting, texting returns | Requires a new port to move back |
| **Best for** | Keeping your phone system as-is | Leaving your current provider entirely |

<Warning>
  **MessageDesk doesn't offer full voice service.** After a port, texting runs through your Inbox, but MessageDesk can't act as your phone system. You have to [set up call forwarding](/settings/workspace-settings/phone-management#call-forwarding) to a number that can answer calls, or inbound callers will have nowhere to land.

  If you need full voice features on the number, port it to a VoIP provider instead and let MessageDesk [host it for texting](/get-started/phone-numbers/landline-voip).
</Warning>

MessageDesk shows a warning banner when you choose the port option, and a final confirmation before the request is submitted. Both are there because porting is difficult to reverse.

## Before you start

<Warning>
  **US numbers need an approved carrier registration campaign first.** If your workspace has no campaign, or your campaign hasn't been approved yet, MessageDesk blocks the port request and prompts you to finish [carrier registration](/carrier-registration/submit).
</Warning>

<Note>
  **Canadian numbers are the exception.** Numbers with a Canadian area code can be ported without an approved campaign. Canada doesn't run on the A2P 10DLC registration system that US numbers require.

  A Canadian number added this way can only text **Canadian recipients** until you complete [carrier registration](/carrier-registration/submit) and associate it with an approved campaign.
</Note>

Have this ready:

- The **phone number** you want to port
- Your **current provider account details**, exactly as they appear on your bill
- The name and title of someone **authorized to sign** on behalf of the account holder
- A **call forwarding destination** for inbound calls after the port completes

## How to port a number to MessageDesk

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Settings" iconType="regular" stepNumber={1} titleSize="h3">
    - Click your profile image in the top right of MessageDesk
    - Click "Settings" from the dropdown menu
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Phone Management" stepNumber={2} titleSize="h3">
    - From the left-hand navigation, select "Phone Management" under Workspace Settings
      - If you don't see `Phone Management` you may not have access. Speak with your account administrator for more details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click `Add Phone Number`" stepNumber={3} titleSize="h3">
    - Click `Add Phone Number` button in the top right corner of the Phone Management screen
    - Select **Port existing phone number**
    - Read the warning banner explaining that porting moves voice as well as texting
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the number and account details" stepNumber={4} titleSize="h3">
    - Enter the phone number you want to port
    - Fill in your current provider's account information
    - Details that don't match your provider's records are the most common cause of a rejected port
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill out and sign the Letter of Authorization" stepNumber={5} titleSize="h3">
    - Complete the authorized-signer fields
    - MessageDesk generates a **PDF Letter of Authorization** from what you enter
    - Sign it using the signature field
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and submit" stepNumber={6} titleSize="h3">
    - A confirmation dialog appears summarizing what porting changes
    - Confirm to submit the request to the carrier
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **You can add at most 2 numbers per day** through porting, hosting, or purchase combined, per workspace. This is a fraud-prevention limit against number-rotation scams. If you need to port more numbers in a day, [contact support](/contact-us).
</Warning>

## After you submit

Track progress from the **Status** column in [Phone Management](/settings/workspace-settings/phone-management#what-the-phone-management-table-shows). Ports take longer than hosting requests because the losing carrier has to release the number.

Once the port completes:

1. **Set up [call forwarding](/settings/workspace-settings/phone-management#call-forwarding)** so inbound calls reach a number that can answer them.
2. Confirm the number shows an active status and a campaign association.
3. [Name the line](/settings/workspace-settings/phone-management#namelabel-phone-lines-and-add-emojis) so your team can identify it in the Inbox.

<Note>
  **Don't cancel your old service until the port completes.** Cancelling early releases the number back to the carrier pool and the port will fail.
</Note>

## FAQ

**How long does a port take?**\
Longer than hosting, and the timeline depends on your losing carrier's release process. Plan for several business days and don't schedule campaigns against the number until it's active.

**Can I port a mobile or Google Voice number?**\
You can port a number out of a mobile carrier or Google Voice into MessageDesk. See [Move a Google Voice Number to MessageDesk](/migration/google-voice) for that path specifically.

**What happens to texts during the port?**\
Texting continues with your current provider until the port completes, then moves to your MessageDesk Inbox.

**Can I undo a port?**\
Not directly. You'd submit a new port request with your new provider to move the number again. This is why MessageDesk asks you to confirm before submitting.

**How do I port a number away from MessageDesk?**\
That process runs in the opposite direction and is driven by your new provider. See [Port your number away from MessageDesk](/settings/workspace-settings/phone-management#port-your-number-away-from-messagedesk).
