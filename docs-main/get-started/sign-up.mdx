# Create your account and sign up for MessageDesk

Create your account, verify your identity, and name your workspace.

---

## Requirements

- **Valid email address**
- **Personal mobile phone number** (for verification; not used as a workspace number)

<Warning>
  Landlines, Google Voice/Skype/virtual numbers **won't** work for verification.

  MessageDesk supports international phone numbers for account creation, login, and two-factor verification only.
</Warning>

---

## 1. Create your account

If you're new (and haven't [been invited to an existing workspace](/get-started/join-workspace)), go to the sign-up page and enter your **email** and **password**, or choose **Sign in with Google**.

![Create Account 1](/images/create-account-1.png "Create Account 1")

**Password must include:**

- At least **1 uppercase** letter
- At least **1 number**
- At least **1 symbol**

---

## Verify via mobile (SMS code)

You'll receive a **6-digit code** by SMS to your **personal mobile number**. Enter the code to continue.

MessageDesk sends 2FA codes from a shortcode, which delivers them faster. You can also add a personal international phone number to receive 2FA login codes.

<Warning>
  **Warning:** Do not enter a non-mobile phone number - this number is used for 2FA codes specifically for personal user login. It is not tied to your MessageDesk workspace. Don't enter a non-mobile phone number. MessageDesk uses this number for 2FA codes for your personal login only. It isn't tied to your MessageDesk workspace.
</Warning>

![Confirm Sign Up Pn](/images/confirm-sign-up.png)

<Warning>
  Didn't get the code? Email [support@messagedesk.com](mailto:support@messagedesk.com) for help.
</Warning>

---

## Verify your email (email code)

Next, we'll send a **second 6-digit code** to your **email**. Enter the code to finish verification.

![Verify Sign In Email Pn](/images/verify-sign-in-email.png)

<Warning>
  Didn't get the email? Check your spam/junk folders, then contact [support@messagedesk.com](mailto:support@messagedesk.com) for help.
</Warning>

---

## Name your workspace

Give your new workspace/inbox a **name** to complete the setup. You can change this later in **Settings**.

![Inbox Name Pn](/images/inbox-name.png)

---

## What happens next?

You'll land in your **new MessageDesk workspace** with a prompt to complete the following steps:

1. [Carrier Registration](/carrier-registration/overview): your MessageDesk account includes this, and it's required when you connect new phone numbers. The process varies depending on the types of phone numbers you connect.
2. [Connect phone numbers](/get-started/phone-numbers/overview): you have several options here.
3. [Invite team members](/settings/workspace-settings/team-management): bring your team to MessageDesk with set user roles and permissions.
4. [Import or add contacts](/contacts/create-import): add contacts manually or via a .csv file upload.

![Welcome Splash 1](/images/welcome-splash-1.png "Welcome Splash 1")

## Frequently asked questions

**Is my personal mobile number used for texting customers?**\
No. MessageDesk only uses it for **account security** and two-factor authentication (2FA). Your workspace uses **separate business numbers** you connect or purchase.

**Can I use an international phone number to create a MessageDesk account?**\
Yes. MessageDesk supports international phone numbers, but only for account creation, login, and two-factor account verification. MessageDesk doesn't support sending or receiving messages from international numbers.

**Can I change my workspace name later?**\
Yes. Update it anytime in **Settings → Workspace Profile**.

**I was invited to a workspace. Do I still use this page?**\
Follow the **invitation link** you received to join the existing workspace: see [**Join a workspace**](/get-started/join-workspace).
