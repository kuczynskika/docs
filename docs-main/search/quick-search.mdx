# Quick Search

Quick Search is the fastest way to find a **contact** or **conversation** in MessageDesk. It lives in the top nav bar on desktop and is built for keyboard-first navigation.

<Note>
  Quick Search is **desktop-only**. On mobile and tablet, tap the search icon near the account settings icon to open [Advanced Search](/search/advanced-search) directly.
</Note>

---

## Open Quick Search

You have two ways to open Quick Search on desktop:

- Press **⌘K** (macOS) or **Ctrl K** (Windows/Linux) from anywhere in the app.
- Click the **search bar** in the top nav.

---

## What Quick Search covers

Quick Search returns results for two item types:

- **Contacts:** saved contact records in your workspace
- **Conversations:** text threads in your inbox

<Note>
  **Messages** and **comments** are **not** in Quick Search results. To search by message content, comment content, or labels, use [Advanced Search](/search/advanced-search).
</Note>

<Note>
  **Search isn't scoped by the phone line you're viewing.** Quick Search always runs across every conversation you have access to, no matter which phone line is selected in the Inbox side nav. To narrow the Inbox itself to a single line, use the side nav or the Inbox **Workspace phone** filter. See [Filter by phone line](#filter-by-phone-line) below.
</Note>

---

## Filter by phone line

Quick Search doesn't have a per-phone-line filter today. To work in the context of a single line, scope the **Inbox** first:

- **Inbox side nav.** Click a specific phone line to see only conversations on that number.
- **Inbox Filter.** Click the **Filter** icon, pick **Workspace phone**, and select one or more numbers. See [Filter & sort conversations](/inbox/filter-search-sort#apply-a-custom-filter-to-your-inbox-view) for details.

When you open a conversation from a search result, its phone line is shown on the conversation's label in the Inbox list and in **Conversation details**.

---

## Recent conversations

Before you type anything, Quick Search shows your **recent conversations** so you can jump back to a thread you were just in. No query required.

---

## Navigate results with the keyboard

- **↑ / ↓:** move between results
- **Enter:** select the highlighted result

Selecting a result with **Enter** opens [Advanced Search](/search/advanced-search) **at that result**. From there you can apply per-type filters.

---

## Open Advanced Search from Quick Search

At the bottom of the Quick Search results dropdown, click **Open Advanced Search** to run your current query in Advanced Search. Use it when you want to search messages, comments, or labels, or apply per-type filters.

You can also click the **search icon** next to any Quick Search result to jump straight into Advanced Search at that result.
