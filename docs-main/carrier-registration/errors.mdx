# Carrier registration errors and how to fix them

Errors fall into four groups: form errors (before you submit), brand verification failures, campaign rejections, and send-time errors (after approval). Each section below explains what the message means and the fastest way to resolve it.

<Note>
  New to registration? Start with the [overview](/carrier-registration/overview) and the [step-by-step submit guide](/carrier-registration/submit). This page is for diagnosing something that's already gone wrong.
</Note>

---

## 1. Form errors (before you can submit)

MessageDesk checks each field before it lets you submit. Fix these in the registration wizard, then resubmit.

| What you'll see | What it means | How to fix it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A tax ID is required to register** | You selected a path that requires an EIN/BN but didn't provide one. | Enter your 9-digit EIN (US) or BN (Canada). Sole proprietorships can't register. See the [overview](/carrier-registration/overview). |
| **Tax ID (EIN or Business Number) must be 9 digits** | The EIN/BN you entered isn't exactly 9 digits. | Remove spaces or dashes and enter the 9 digits exactly as they appear on your IRS/CRA records. |
| **Required: enter your legal name** | Legal company name is blank. | Enter your legal entity name exactly as filed (include the LLC/Inc suffix). |
| **Please select an entity type / business vertical / country / state** | A required dropdown is empty. | Choose the value that matches your tax filings. LLCs taxed as S-Corps should still select **LLC**. |
| **Required: a valid support email / support phone** | Public support contact is missing or malformed. | Enter a working public-facing email and phone. Both appear on carrier records. |
| **Campaign description must be at least 40 characters** | Your description is too short for carriers to review. | Use **Generate with AI**, or describe who you text and what you send in a full sentence or two. |
| **Select at least one group / message type** | No recipients or message types are checked. | Check every group you'll text and every message type you'll send. |
| **Please select your estimated volume** | Monthly volume isn't set. | Pick the tier that matches your expected monthly send. |
| **At least one sample message is required** / **Required: N valid sample messages** | You have too few valid samples. | Provide **3 to 5** sample messages. Each must be 20–320 characters. |
| **Every message must include opt-out language** | A sample is missing STOP language. | Add "Reply STOP to opt out" (or equivalent) to every sample message. |
| **Your samples must match what you described in your campaign** | Samples don't align with your campaign description. | Rewrite samples so they clearly reflect the use cases you selected. Mismatches are the #1 rejection reason. |
| **At least one sample must include a URL** / **a phone number** | You declared that messages contain links/numbers, but no sample shows one. | Add a real link and/or phone number to at least one sample, or turn off that toggle if it doesn't apply. |
| **A sample link is required when messages include URLs** / **Sample link must be a valid URL** | The declared sample link is missing or invalid. | Provide a complete, working URL (including `https://`). |
| **SMS Terms & Conditions page is missing or incomplete** | Carriers couldn't find compliant SMS terms on your site. | Publish SMS terms and conditions and link them from your footer. See [privacy policy requirements](/carrier-registration/privacy-policy-terms-conditions). |
| **Privacy policy is missing the required no-sharing statement** | Your policy lacks a "we don't sell or share" clause. | Add a statement that SMS opt-in data isn't sold or shared with third parties for marketing. |
| **You must confirm this to continue / to submit** | A required acknowledgement (e.g. forbidden topics) is unchecked. | Check the confirmation box to proceed. |

<Note>
  Field errors appear inline in the wizard and in the **Review** step's missing-field summary. You can save progress and return; nothing submits until every required field passes.
</Note>

## 2. Brand verification failures (after you submit)

After you submit, carriers verify your **brand** (your business identity) against IRS or CRA records. If it can't be verified, your brand shows as **Identity failed** in the Compliance Center.

<Warning>
  **Most identity failures are a data mismatch.** Your EIN is matched byte-for-byte against IRS/CRA records. If your legal name, EIN, or address doesn't match exactly, verification fails.
</Warning>

**Common causes and fixes:**

- **Legal name mismatch.** Match your registered legal name exactly, including the suffix (LLC, Inc, Corp).
- **EIN mismatch.** Confirm the 9-digit EIN against your IRS Letter 147C or SS-4 confirmation.
- **Address mismatch.** Use the address on your tax filings, including suite/unit.
- **Brand-new EIN.** If your EIN is less than ~30 days old, carrier records may not have propagated. Wait about 30 days and resubmit.

When a brand fails, MessageDesk reopens it for editing so you can correct and resubmit. When carriers return specific feedback (for example, a tax-ID correction), MessageDesk surfaces it on the registration so you know exactly what to fix.

<Note>
  A brand in **Pending**, **Unverified**, or **Self-declared** status is still in review. It isn't yet approved and hasn't failed. You don't need to take action unless it moves to **Identity failed**.
</Note>

## 3. Campaign rejections and suspensions

Once your brand is verified, your **campaign** (what you send) goes to the carriers. It can come back **Declined** or **Suspended**.

**Declined** means carriers rejected the campaign. Common reasons:

- **Vague or mismatched samples.** Samples don't match the campaign description. Rewrite them to line up.
- **Forbidden content.** Campaigns tied to prohibited topics are rejected immediately and may lead to permanent blacklisting. Review the [forbidden topics](/carrier-registration/submit) list.
- **Missing consent or opt-out.** Document your opt-in method and include STOP language.
- **Website issues.** Your site must be live and show the same legal name/address, with an accessible privacy policy and SMS terms.

**Suspended** means carriers paused an active campaign, usually for a compliance or content concern. Contact [support@messagedesk.com](mailto:support@messagedesk.com) to review and restore it.

<Warning>
  Corrections and resubmissions reset the review clock and add at least another 48 hours per resubmission. Fix everything before you resubmit.
</Warning>

MessageDesk reopens declined and suspended campaigns so you can correct and resubmit. If your brand is denied, any pending campaigns move back to **Draft**.

## 4. Errors when sending after approval

Even after approval, you may see these when sending. They relate to registration status, not the message itself.

| Message | What it means | How to fix it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Phone number is not registered, please register number to send messages** | The number isn't tied to an approved campaign. | Finish registration, or contact support to associate the number with your campaign. |
| **Number is pending association with campaign, this can take up to 24 hours** | Your number is approved but still linking to the campaign. | Wait up to 24 hours. No action needed. |
| **Registration has been suspended by the carrier network** | Carriers suspended your campaign. | Contact [support@messagedesk.com](mailto:support@messagedesk.com) to review and restore. |
| **Number has exceeded messaging limits** | You hit your campaign's daily or per-second send limit. | Slow your send rate, or contact support about a higher throughput tier (may require enhanced vetting). |

<Note>
  Sending more than **2,000 messages/day** requires enhanced carrier vetting and may incur additional fees.
</Note>

## Registration status glossary

You'll see these statuses in **Account → Settings → Compliance Center**.

| Status | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Not yet submitted. | Complete and submit. |
| **Submitted / Pending** | In review by MessageDesk or carriers. | Wait. Typically resolves within 24–48 hours per stage. |
| **Identity verified** | Your brand passed identity checks. | None. Campaign review comes next. |
| **Identity failed** | Brand couldn't be verified. | Correct your business details and resubmit (see section 2). |
| **Active** | Campaign approved. You can send. | None. Stay compliant with your registered use case. |
| **Declined** | Campaign rejected by carriers. | Fix the issue and resubmit (see section 3). |
| **Suspended** | Active campaign paused by carriers. | Contact support. |
