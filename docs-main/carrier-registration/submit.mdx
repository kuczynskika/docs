# How to submit your carrier registration with MessageDesk

Carrier registration is **built into** MessageDesk's phone setup flow. When you configure phone lines, you'll provide your business details, choose how you want to connect numbers, and submit registration. It all happens in one place.

---

## What you'll need to complete carrier registration (quick checklist)

- **Valid payment method**
- **Legal business name** (exactly as filed, incl. LLC/Inc)
- **EIN** (you can provide your EIN via email; tax documents are only required if manual verification is needed) and **business address**
- **Website or public listing** (Facebook, Yelp, etc.)
- **Use-case description** (what you'll send)
- **Three sample SMS messages** (include "Reply **STOP** to opt out" in at least one)
- **Consent method** (web form checkbox, written/verbal, keyword)

<Warning>
  **Sole proprietorships aren't supported.** MessageDesk doesn't currently register sole proprietorships for business texting. To submit a registration, your business must be a registered legal entity (LLC, Inc, Corp, non-profit, government, and so on) with an EIN. See the [Carrier Registration Overview](/carrier-registration/overview) for options.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Note:** While your registration is pending, you can't send or receive messages in MessageDesk.
</Warning>

## How to begin carrier registration in MessageDesk

You can begin the carrier registration process in several ways when logged in to MessageDesk:

### 1. Visit Account -\> Settings

Navigate to **Compliance Center → click on the "Registration tab" to begin**.

<Frame>
  ![Compliance Center Registration](/images/compliance-center-registration.png "Compliance Center Registration")
</Frame>

### 2. Begin carrier registration

Click on Start registration to begin your carrier registration. 

Before you can start sending text messages, you need to register your business (brand) and campaign with the carrier network. The submission process takes about 10 minutes, and your progress is saved as you go.

<Warning>
  **Note:** you'll need to start a **14-day free trial** and **enter a valid payment method** to submit a carrier registration request. MessageDesk begins billing your account at the conclusion of your 14-day free trial.
</Warning>

### 3. Start

Answer a few questions regarding the geographic location of your message recipients, your tax status, and the types of numbers you want to use in MessageDesk to send text messages.

#### Where are the people you'll be texting?

USA, Canada, or both. Your answer determines which carrier regulations apply to your messaging. All US messaging requires full A2P 10DLC carrier registration. MessageDesk users only messaging CA recipients are currently exempt from carrier registration, but additional rules and regulations may apply.

#### Do you have a tax ID (EIN or BN)?

In the US, your EIN is a 9-digit number issued by the IRS. In Canada, your BN is a 9-digit number issued by the CRA. Carriers require tax identification to verify your identity.

#### What numbers will you use to send messages?

You can use local 10-digit (10DLC) and toll-free numbers with MessageDesk.

<Warning>
  **Note:** Toll-free numbers (800, 888, etc.) have a separate registration process. To complete a toll-free carrier registration, email **support@messagedesk.com**.
</Warning>

### 4. Enter your business information

Begin by selecting the county that issued your tax ID (EIN/USA or BN/Canada).

<Note>

  What's a W-9? [Click to view an example.](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fw9.pdf)

  <br />Where can I find my Canadian BN? [Click for info.](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/taxes/business-number.html)
</Note>

#### Organization identity

Enter the following:

- **Legal company name:** Must match your IRS or CRA records. Mismatches cause registration delays.
- **DBA/display name:** Toggle this on and enter your brand name if it differs from the legal name on file with the IRS.
- **Entity type (partnership, LLC, corporation, non-profit, government)**: Must match your tax filings. LLCs taxed as S-Corps should still select LLC.
- **EIN/Tax ID**: Your 9-digit IRS Employer Identification Number. Found on your IRS Letter 147C or SS-4 confirmation.
- **Industry/vertical:** Carriers use your industry vertical for risk scoring. Certain verticals like Cannabis or Gambling face extra scrutiny.

#### Mailing address

- Street address
- City
- State/provice
- ZIP/postal code

<Warning>
  **Note:** Publicly traded companies must complete enhanced carrier vetting through MessageDesk.
</Warning>

#### Online presence

Enter your business website. If you don't have one, a Facebook or Instagram business-page link is also acceptable. We use this link to scan your website for privacy policy and SMS terms and conditions language.

#### Support contact (email and phone)

Enter a public-facing email and phone number where contacts can reach your organization for support. Both the email and phone will appear on carrier records.

### 6. Acknowledge forbidden messaging topics

Carriers restrict messaging in the following content categories. Messaging campaigns pertaining to forbidden content are immediately rejected and may result in permanent blacklisting. 

<Frame>
  ![Carrier Registration Forbidden Topics](/images/carrier-registration-forbidden-topics.png "Carrier Registration Forbidden Topics")
</Frame>

#### Cannabis / CBD / THC 

Messages promoting, selling, or advertising cannabis, CBD, THC, or related products are prohibited regardless of state or local legality. 

#### Firearms, Weapons & Ammunition 

Content promoting the sale of firearms, ammunition, explosives, or weapons (including accessories) is forbidden. 

#### Gambling & Betting 

Unsolicited messages related to gambling, sports betting, casinos, lotteries, or sweepstakes are not permitted. 

#### Adult / Sexual Content 

Any sexually explicit material, adult entertainment services, escort services, or dating services with sexual content are prohibited. 

#### Hate Speech & Harassment 

Messages containing hate speech, threats, harassment, bullying, or content that discriminates based on protected classes are forbidden. 

#### Phishing, Fraud & Deceptive Practices 

Deceptive messages designed to fraudulently obtain personal information, impersonate entities, or mislead recipients are strictly prohibited. ### Controlled Substances & Prescription Drugs Promotion of illegal drugs, unauthorized pharmaceuticals, or unapproved supplements is not allowed. 

#### Predatory Lending & Debt Collection Abuse 

High-interest lending solicitations (payday loans), abusive debt collection tactics, or misleading financial offers are forbidden. 

#### Unsanctioned Political / PAC Messages 

Political messaging that lacks proper sender identification, opt-in consent, or violates campaign finance disclosure requirements. 

#### Other Illegal Activities 

Any content that promotes, facilitates, or encourages illegal activity under federal, state, or local law. 

#### **Does your messaging include age-restricted content?** e.g., alcohol & tobacco

<Warning>
  **Note:** Age-restricted industries can still register. Businesses like alcohol distributors, bars, and restaurants are eligible for registration, but you need age-gating on your website before you can register. A rep will walk you through what's needed. Email [support@messagedesk.com](mailto:support@messagedesk.com). We'll tell you exactly what you need for your vertical. 
</Warning>

#### **Does your business offer lending, loans, or credit products?** e.g., loans, credit, or other financial services 

<Warning>
  **Note: Lending & credit businesses can still register**. We can support businesses that offer lending, loans, or credit products, but these carry extra carrier requirements. A rep will walk you through what you need before you register. 

  Email [support@messagedesk.com](mailto:support@messagedesk.com) first, and we'll tell you exactly what you need for your vertical. 
</Warning>

#### Confirmation: 

Confirm that your messaging campaigns will not include content related to any of the forbidden topics listed above.

### 7. Add sample messages

Write example text messages your contacts would actually receive. Vague or mismatched sample messages unrelated to your campaign description are the number one reason for campaign rejections.

<Frame>
  ![Carrier Registration Sample Messages](/images/carrier-registration-sample-messages.png "Carrier Registration Sample Messages")
</Frame>

#### Will your messages ever contain links or phone numbers?

Toggle one or both of these on if your messages will contain links or phone numbers. Declaring these accurately matters. Carriers check your sample messages against what you declare here.

#### What carriers check in your sample messages

- Write texts your contacts would actually receive. Not placeholders.
- Include your org name in every message.
- Every message must include opt-out language (e.g. "Reply STOP to opt out"). 
- Your sample messages must match what you described in your campaign description. Carriers reject mismatches.

## After you submit your carrier registration

- **Status updates:** Our team will contact you directly within 24-48 hours once you submit your registration request.
- **Connect with Support:** Questions? Email [**support@messagedesk.com**](mailto:support@messagedesk.com)<br />or text **(775) 313-9465** for a faster response.
- **Estimated completion time:**
  - Normal business/organization registration with an EIN: ~**1–3 business days (sometimes within 24 hours)**
  - Toll-Free carrier registrations: ~**1–2 weeks**
- **Sending while pending:** Until you complete carrier registration and configure your phone numbers, you can't send or receive text messages

## Tips to speed approval

- Make sure your **website shows the same legal name** and address you submit.
- Include **opt-out language** ("Reply STOP…") in at least one **sample message**.
- Describe **exactly** what you plan to send (e.g., "appointment reminders and service updates").
- For toll-free, submit **one form per number** to verify each line.
