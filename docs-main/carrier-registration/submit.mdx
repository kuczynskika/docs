# How to submit your carrier registration with MessageDesk

Carrier registration is built into MessageDesk's phone setup flow. When you configure phone lines, you provide your business details, choose how you want to connect numbers, and submit your registration from the same place.

---

## What you'll need to complete carrier registration (quick checklist)

- **Valid payment method**
- **Legal business name** (exactly as filed, including LLC/Inc)
- **EIN** (you can provide your EIN via email. Tax documents are only required if manual verification is needed) and **business address**
- **Website or public listing** (Facebook, Yelp, etc.)
- **Use-case description** (what you'll send)
- **3 to 5 sample SMS messages** (include "Reply **STOP** to opt out" in at least one)
- **Consent method** (web form checkbox, written or verbal, keyword)

<Warning>
  **Sole proprietorships aren't supported.** MessageDesk doesn't currently register sole proprietorships for business texting. To submit a registration, your business must be a registered legal entity (LLC, Inc, Corp, non-profit, government, and so on) with an EIN. See the [Carrier Registration Overview](/carrier-registration/overview) for options.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  While your registration is pending, you can't send text messages in MessageDesk.
</Warning>

## Carrier registration timeline

1. **(10 min.)** Submit the required carrier registration information via MessageDesk 
   1. Website changes, including changes to your privacy policy, SMS terms and conditions, and opt-in method (website forms, etc.), **must be complete** before you can submit your carrier registration.
2. **(24 hours)** MessageDesk reviews your submitted brand and campaign information during normal business days (excluding holidays and weekends).
   1. You can view the status of your campaign at any time in the compliance dashboard by visiting **Account → Settings → Compliance Center**.
3. **(48 hours)** Upstream carrier providers review and either approve or deny your registration

<Warning>
  This timeline assumes no errors or inaccuracies in the carrier registration you submit. Campaign corrections and resubmissions reset the registration clock back to zero and add at least another 48 hours for each resubmission and review.
</Warning>

## How to begin carrier registration in MessageDesk

You can begin the carrier registration process several ways when logged in to MessageDesk.

### 1. Go to Account -\> Settings

Go to the **Compliance Center** and click the **Registration** tab to begin.

<Frame>
  ![Compliance Center Registration](/images/compliance-center-registration.png "Compliance Center Registration")
</Frame>

### 2. Begin carrier registration

Click **Start registration** to begin.

Before you can send text messages, you need to register your business (brand) and campaign with the carrier network. The submission takes about 10 minutes, and MessageDesk saves your progress as you go.

<Warning>
  You'll need to start a **14-day free trial** and **enter a valid payment method** to submit a carrier registration request. MessageDesk begins billing your account at the end of your 14-day free trial.
</Warning>

### 3. Start

Answer a few questions about where your message recipients are, your tax status, and the types of numbers you want to use in MessageDesk.

#### Where are the people you'll be texting?

USA, Canada, or both. Your answer determines which carrier regulations apply to your messaging. All U.S. messaging requires full A2P 10DLC carrier registration. MessageDesk users who only message Canadian recipients are currently exempt from carrier registration, but additional rules may apply.

#### Do you have a tax ID (EIN or BN)?

In the U.S., your EIN is a 9-digit number issued by the IRS. In Canada, your BN is a 9-digit number issued by the CRA. Carriers require a tax ID to verify your identity.

#### What numbers will you use to send messages?

You can use local 10-digit (10DLC) and toll-free numbers with MessageDesk.

<Warning>
  Toll-free numbers (800, 888, etc.) have a separate registration process. To complete a toll-free carrier registration, email [**support@messagedesk.com**](mailto:support@messagedesk.com).
</Warning>

### 6. Acknowledge forbidden messaging topics

Carriers restrict messaging in the following content categories. Carriers immediately reject messaging campaigns tied to forbidden content, and repeat violations may result in permanent blacklisting.

<Frame>
  ![Carrier Registration Forbidden Topics](/images/carrier-registration-forbidden-topics.png "Carrier Registration Forbidden Topics")
</Frame>

#### Cannabis / CBD / THC

Messages promoting, selling, or advertising cannabis, CBD, THC, or related products are prohibited regardless of state or local legality.

#### Firearms, weapons, and ammunition

Content promoting the sale of firearms, ammunition, explosives, or weapons (including accessories) is forbidden.

#### Gambling and betting

Unsolicited messages related to gambling, sports betting, casinos, lotteries, or sweepstakes aren't permitted.

#### Adult / sexual content

Any sexually explicit material, adult entertainment services, escort services, or dating services with sexual content is prohibited.

#### Hate speech and harassment

Messages containing hate speech, threats, harassment, bullying, or content that discriminates based on protected classes are forbidden.

#### Phishing, fraud, and deceptive practices

Deceptive messages designed to fraudulently obtain personal information, impersonate entities, or mislead recipients are strictly prohibited.

#### Controlled substances and prescription drugs

Promotion of illegal drugs, unauthorized pharmaceuticals, or unapproved supplements isn't allowed.

#### Predatory lending and debt collection abuse

High-interest lending solicitations (payday loans), abusive debt collection tactics, and misleading financial offers are forbidden.

#### Unsanctioned political / PAC messages

Political messaging that lacks proper sender identification, opt-in consent, or violates campaign finance disclosure requirements.

#### Other illegal activities

Any content that promotes, facilitates, or encourages illegal activity under federal, state, or local law.

#### **Does your messaging include age-restricted content?** e.g., alcohol and tobacco

<Warning>
  Age-restricted industries can still register. Businesses like alcohol distributors, bars, and restaurants are eligible, but you need age-gating on your website first. A rep will walk you through what's needed. Email [support@messagedesk.com](mailto:support@messagedesk.com). We'll tell you exactly what you need for your vertical.
</Warning>

#### **Does your business offer lending, loans, or credit products?** e.g., loans, credit, or other financial services

<Warning>
  **Lending and credit businesses can still register.** We can support businesses that offer lending, loans, or credit products, but these carry extra carrier requirements. A rep will walk you through what you need before you register.

  Email [support@messagedesk.com](mailto:support@messagedesk.com) first, and we'll tell you exactly what you need for your vertical.
</Warning>

#### Confirmation:

Confirm that your messaging campaigns won't include content related to any of the forbidden topics listed above.

### 7. Add sample messages

Write example text messages your contacts would actually receive. Vague or mismatched sample messages that don't line up with your campaign description are the number one reason for campaign rejections.

<Note>
  Provide **3 to 5** sample messages. Three is the minimum; five is the maximum carriers accept. Each message must be 20–320 characters. If you declared that your messages include links or phone numbers, **at least one** sample must contain a real link, and at least one must contain a real phone number.
</Note>

<Frame>
  ![Carrier Registration Sample Messages](/images/carrier-registration-sample-messages.png "Carrier Registration Sample Messages")
</Frame>

#### Will your messages ever contain links or phone numbers?

Toggle one or both of these on if your messages will contain links or phone numbers. Declaring these accurately matters. Carriers check your sample messages against what you declare here.

#### What carriers check in your sample messages

- Provide **at least 3** samples (up to 5). Each must be 20–320 characters.
- Write texts your contacts would actually receive. Not placeholders.
- Include your org name in every message.
- Every message must include opt-out language (for example, "Reply STOP to opt out").
- Your sample messages must match what you described in your campaign description. Carriers reject mismatches.

#### Click Generate with AI

Click to create carrier-compliant sample messages based on your campaign description.

### 8. Website privacy policy and SMS terms and conditions

Your website needs an SMS-compliant privacy policy and a callout for specific, carrier-required, SMS terms and conditions. Carriers visit your website and check your privacy policy and SMS terms before approving your carrier registration campaign.

<Frame>
  ![Carrier Registration Privacy Terms](/images/carrier-registration-privacy-terms.png "Carrier Registration Privacy Terms")
</Frame>

What you'll need:

- Access to your website (or the name of who manages it)
- Your privacy policy and SMS terms page links

#### Website URL

Enter your website URL. We use AI to scan your site for a privacy policy and SMS terms and conditions. This helps us personalize the suggested language to add to your website.

#### Policy contact information

Enter the email and phone number for the contact info generated in the privacy policy and SMS terms text below.

#### 1. Privacy Policy and 2. SMS Terms and Conditions

Your website needs an SMS-compliant privacy policy and SMS terms and conditions. Carriers visit your site and check both before approving your campaign.

At a minimum, your privacy policy must state how you use phone numbers, give opt-out instructions (**"Reply STOP to opt out"**), and confirm that opt-in data **isn't sold or shared** with third parties for marketing. Your SMS terms must cover message frequency, opt-out, HELP, contact info, and that message and data rates may apply.

<Note>
  As a best practice, link your privacy policy and SMS terms from your website **footer**. This language must be present and accessible to carriers as part of the vetting process.
</Note>

<Note>
  Full, carrier-ready example privacy-policy and SMS terms language — plus a downloadable template and a free AI checker — lives on the [SMS Privacy Policy Requirements](/carrier-registration/privacy-policy-terms-conditions) page. Copy it from there so you're always using the current version.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **This isn't legal advice.** MessageDesk isn't a law firm. The template language is general information to help you meet carrier (CTIA / The Campaign Registry) registration requirements and may not fit your business. Review changes with your own legal counsel, and make sure your policies accurately describe your actual data practices. You're responsible for your website's content and its compliance with applicable law.
</Warning>

<Warning>

## Tips to speed approval

- Make sure your **website shows the same legal name** and address you submit.
- Include **opt-out language** ("Reply STOP…") in at least one **sample message**.
- Describe **exactly** what you plan to send (for example, "appointment reminders and service updates").
- For toll-free, submit **one form per number** to verify each line.
