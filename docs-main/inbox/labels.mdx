# Organize & Group Conversations With Labels

Labels let you sort, find, and manage conversations fast without building complicated folder structures.

A label is a small badge made of **text + color + icon** that you can apply to any conversation.

---

## Why use labels

- **Stay organized.** Group related conversations across teams and topics.
- **Work faster.** Scan your inbox and spot priorities by color/icon.
- **Report & review.** Filter by label to see everything for a project, region, or status.
- **Scope visibility.** Use labels as a permission boundary. Custom roles can be set to Editor, Viewer, or No Access on specific **Label inboxes**. See [Custom roles and permissions](/settings/workspace-settings/team-management#custom-roles-and-permissions).
- **Keep it simple.** Add or remove labels anytime. No impact on message delivery.

<Note>
  You can apply **multiple labels** to the same conversation.
</Note>

---

## Common label ideas (by team)

**Logistics / Dispatch**

- **Drivers.** Conversations with field staff.
- **Region: North** / **Region: South.** Geographic routing.
- **Urgent.** Time-sensitive jobs.

**HR**

- **Benefits.** Questions and announcements.
- **New hire.** Onboarding conversations.

**Support / Success**

- **Support.** Active cases.
- **Review-ready.** Happy customers to invite for a review.
- **Bug.** Product issues to track.

**Sales / Ops**

- **Proposal.** Outstanding quotes.
- **Order.** Order updates.
- **Won** / **Lost.** Quick stage markers.

<Tip>
  Use a short **prefix** for clarity (`HR: New hire`, `Sales: Proposal`, `Support: Bug`).
</Tip>

## Label a conversation (from the Inbox)

1. Open a conversation.
2. Click the **Label** icon (top-right of the thread).
3. Check one or more labels to apply them.
4. Close the menu. The labels appear above the thread and in the conversation list.

**Remove a label.** Reopen the **Label** menu and uncheck it.

<Note>
  Labels without a custom icon show a placeholder label icon in the menu.
</Note>

![Inbox Labels Menu Pn](/images/inbox-labels-menu.png)

After you label a conversation, you'll see every label on that conversation in the Inbox list.

![Inbox Labeled Conversations Pn](/images/inbox-labeled-conversations.png)

## Create & edit labels (quickly from the message thread)

1. Open a conversation, then click the **Label** icon.
2. Click **Create label** (or **Edit labels**).
3. Set **Name**, **Color**, and **Icon**, then click **Save**.

<Tip>
  Keep names short and scannable. Prefer `Proposal – ACME` over `All proposals currently outstanding for ACME Corp`.
</Tip>

## Create & manage labels (Workspace Settings)

Admins can manage labels for everyone.

1. Go to **Account → Settings**.
2. Under **Workspace Settings**, open **Inbox Settings → Labels**.
3. **Create**, **rename**, **change color/icon**, or **remove** labels.

<Tip>
  **Multi-select** labels and **delete them in bulk** from Label Settings. No need to remove them one at a time.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Labels sort **alphabetically (A–Z)** by name across label menus, settings, and filters. To control where a label appears, rename it (add a prefix like `HR:` or `Support:`).
</Note>

<Warning>
  Renaming a label updates it **everywhere** it's used.
</Warning>

## Find conversations by label

- Open your **Inbox** and use the **filter/search** to show conversations with a specific label.
- Save common filters (`Support + Urgent`) as a **view** so your team can jump in fast.

## Label naming & color conventions

- **Keep it short.** 1–2 words, or a word plus emoji/icon.
- **Use prefixes.** `HR:`, `Ops:`, `Sales:`, `Support:`.
- **Color with intent.**
  - Green = success/ready
  - Yellow = waiting/attention
  - Red = urgent/blocker
- **Avoid duplicates.** One meaning per label name.

**Examples**

- `Support: Urgent`
- `Sales: Proposal`
- `Ops: Region – West`
- `HR: New hire`
- `Review-ready`

## Apply labels to contacts

Labels are applied to **conversations, not directly to contacts**. How you apply a label depends on whether you've already started a conversation with the contact.

### If the contact doesn't have a conversation yet

You can't apply a label directly to a contact if no conversation exists. Instead, apply the label when sending your first message:

1. Start a [new message](/messaging/new-message-compose) to the contact.
2. Select **Add Action** in the New Message Compose screen.
3. Choose the label or labels you want to apply.
4. Send your message.

The selected labels are applied to the new conversation.

<Tip>
  **Messaging multiple contacts at once:** If you're sending the same message to multiple contacts, use **Add Action** to select one or more labels before sending. Those labels are applied to each of the new conversations created by the message. See [post-send actions](/messaging/new-message-compose#post-send-actions).
</Tip>

### If the contact already has a conversation

You have two options:

1. **Apply the label when sending a message.** Select **Add Action** in the New Message Compose screen and choose the label or labels you want to apply.
2. **Apply labels from the Inbox.** Select one or multiple conversations in your Inbox and apply the appropriate labels to them. See [Multi-select & quick actions](/inbox/multi-select-quick-actions).

### Automatically applying labels

You can also use a [Relay](/relays/overview) to automatically apply labels when a specific action or event occurs. This is helpful if you regularly need conversations labeled based on the same workflow.

## Labels vs. contact filters

Labels and contact filters serve different purposes in Inbox by MessageDesk.

- **Labels organize conversations.** They can be applied when sending a message, directly from the Inbox, or automatically using a [Relay](/relays/overview).
- **Contact filters organize contacts.** You can filter contacts based on properties such as [custom fields](/settings/workspace-settings/custom-fields) and save those filters for future use. A [saved filter](/contacts/saved-filters) effectively gives you a reusable group of contacts that you can select and message together.

When sending a message to contacts from a filter, you can also use **Add Action** to apply one or more labels. Those labels are applied to each conversation created by the message.

## FAQ

**Can I apply more than one label to a conversation?**\
Yes. Apply as many as you need.

**Can I apply a label directly to a contact?**\
No. Labels apply to conversations, not contacts. If the contact has no conversation yet, apply the label while sending your first message with **Add Action**. If a conversation exists, label it from the Inbox or when sending a message. See [Apply labels to contacts](#apply-labels-to-contacts).

**What's the difference between labels and contact filters?**\
Labels organize conversations in your Inbox. Contact filters organize contacts, and a [saved filter](/contacts/saved-filters) gives you a reusable group of contacts to message together. See [Labels vs. contact filters](#labels-vs-contact-filters).

**Do labels affect who can see a conversation or whether messages send?**\
Applying or removing a label doesn't by itself grant or revoke anyone's access, and it never affects deliverability. But labels are a scopable resource in the permission model. A custom role can be set to **Editor**, **Viewer**, or **No Access** on specific **Label inboxes**, so an admin can restrict a role to one label and hide the rest. See [Custom roles and permissions](/settings/workspace-settings/team-management#custom-roles-and-permissions).

Two things to know if you scope visibility this way:

- **Assignment overrides label scope.** Assigning a teammate to a conversation grants them Editor on that thread even when they have No Access to its labels. This is what makes Relay routing work, and it also means a misrouted thread is visible to whoever it landed on.
- **Label scope isn't a boundary on its own.** Pair it with **Phone number inbox** permissions on the same role, and test with a real teammate login before relying on it.

**Can I edit or delete a label later?**\
Yes. Edit the name/color/icon anytime from **Inbox (Label menu)** or **Workspace Settings**. Deleting a label removes it from every conversation. You can also multi-select and delete multiple labels at once from **Label Settings**.

**Can I filter by more than one label at a time?**\
Yes. Combine labels in a filter to narrow your view (`Support` + `Urgent`).

**Can I label an anonymous conversation?**\
Yes. Anonymous conversations (phone numbers not saved as contacts) can be labeled the same way as identified ones, both manually from the Label menu and automatically through [Relays](/relays/overview). Labels stay on the conversation if the anonymous number later becomes a saved contact.

## Troubleshooting

- **I don't see the Label icon.**\
  Make sure you're inside a conversation, not just the inbox list. If it's still missing, check your workspace permissions.
- **I created a label, but teammates can't find it.**\
  Ask them to refresh the inbox. Confirm you created it in **Workspace Settings** if it should be available to everyone.
- **We have too many similar labels.**\
  Standardize names with prefixes, merge duplicates (rename to the preferred label, then archive the old one), and document your naming rules in a short internal note.
